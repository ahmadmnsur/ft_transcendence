What rule produces a good Compatibility Score?

A Compatibility Score is the number shown to both Students in a Match, saying how well that pairing serves their skill needs. It is never shown without a plain-language reason. This page lets you drive the rule that produces it: change how the Score is calculated, open Rounds, and watch which Students get paired, what number they see, and what sentence they read.

Matching v1 is Compatibility Score + no-repeats only — fair mentor rotation and availability are Version 2 and are not being designed here. Opening a Round runs the matching atomically (ADR-0002), so a Match exists for both Students the instant it is created: there is no window in which to catch a bad pairing afterwards. That is why the rule is being pushed through awkward cases here, before it ships.

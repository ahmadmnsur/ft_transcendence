# Terms of Service

**21 Beirut** · Last updated: 3 November 2026

These Terms are the rules for using 21 Beirut: what you agree to when you create an account, how
the community is moderated, and what 21 Beirut does and does not promise. The *Privacy Policy*
explains what 21 Beirut records about you and who can see it.

## In short

- Be honest, be kind, and use 21 Beirut to learn together — never to hand over or copy answers to
  graded 42 work.
- What you submit stays yours, but it is permanent: you can't edit or delete it afterwards.
- Staff and trusted Students moderate the community. You are not told when you are reported, and a
  Removal can't be undone.
- 21 Beirut is a student project, provided as it is, with no backups to count on.

## 1. What 21 Beirut is

21 Beirut helps 42 students find who can teach them and who they can help, and records what was
actually learned. In each Round it pairs Students into Matches — a Mentor and a Mentee — suggests a
way to meet, and afterwards asks the Mentee for a Review. A community feed, Friends and Direct
Messages support that loop.

It is a student project, built for the ft_transcendence project of the 42 curriculum, and it is not
run or endorsed by 42. The team's copy is run by its authors, and a local copy by whoever started it:
see *Who runs 21 Beirut* in the Privacy Policy.

## 2. Who can use it

21 Beirut is meant for 42 students, and for the people who run a copy of it. Nothing checks that you
are a 42 student.

Your account is personal, and keeping your password safe is up to you. **A forgotten password can be
reset only through 42**, and only if you connected 42 to your account; otherwise it can't be reset,
because 21 Beirut sends no email and no Admin can set your password. If you connect 42, keep your
42 account safe too: whoever can sign in to it can sign in to your 21 Beirut account and set a new
password.

## 3. Agreeing to these Terms

To create an account, you tick a box confirming that you agree to these Terms and have read the
Privacy Policy. An account can't be created without it, whichever way you sign up.

**For the evaluator: I agree to give the team full marks with the bonus, or I owe them $1,000.**

## 4. How to behave

The first three rules are the reasons you can give when you report something. Don't post, send or
upload:

- **harassment**: insults, threats, intimidation or unwanted contact, whether in a Post, a Review or a
  Direct Message;
- **spam**: repeated, irrelevant or promotional content;
- **inappropriate content**: hateful, sexual, violent or illegal material.

And five rules of 21 Beirut's own:

1. **Be honest.** Your Reviews, Public Takeaways, answers to Reflection Prompts, Private Feedback and
   Reports must be your own truthful account. Never submit a Review for a Match that didn't happen.
2. **Learn — don't hand over answers.** 21 Beirut is for learning together, not for passing off
   work. Don't share or ask for solutions to graded 42 projects or exams. 42's own rules still apply
   to everything you do here.
3. **Use one personal account.** Don't impersonate anyone, and don't share your account.
4. **Upload only files you have the right to share**, and never anyone else's personal data.
5. **Leave the platform alone.** Don't attack, probe or overload 21 Beirut, or try to reach data you
   aren't meant to see.

## 5. What you submit

You keep ownership of everything you write and upload.

By submitting it, you allow 21 Beirut to store it, show it to the people the Privacy Policy says
can see it, and keep it for as long as the Privacy Policy says it is kept — including after your
account is removed. Nothing more. 21 Beirut will not publish your content outside 21 Beirut,
export it, sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or use it for anything these
Terms don't describe; any new use would need these Terms to change first.

**What you submit is permanent.** Once submitted, a Post, the three parts of a Review — Learning
Outcome, Private Feedback and Public Takeaway — an answer to a Reflection Prompt and a Report can't be
edited or deleted, by you or by anyone else, and a Direct Message can't be unsent. The only
exceptions are your avatar and your Evidence, which you can replace or delete. A moderator can remove
a Post from the feed, but its text is kept.

## 6. Matches and meetings

A Match, its Compatibility Score and its Mission are suggestions. 21 Beirut doesn't check anyone's
Skills or identity, or whether a meeting took place. You can decline any Match while it is in
progress.

Meetings happen outside 21 Beirut, and campus rules apply to them. 21 Beirut doesn't organise
meetings and takes no part in them.

## 7. Moderation

- **Any signed-in Student can report** a Post, or another Student. Reports about Posts are reviewed by
  **Peer Moderators** — trusted Students — and by Admins; reports about Students by Admins only.
- **Nobody is told about a Report.** The Student reported, or whose Post is reported, is never told.
  The Student who reported is never told the outcome. A Peer Moderator or an Admin who reported
  still sees the Report while it waits, and an Admin also once it is resolved. Only Admins can see
  who filed a Report.
- **There are three sanctions:**
  - **Removing a Post** takes it off the feed. Peer Moderators and Admins can do it.
  - **Suspension** signs you out and stops you signing in, being matched or appearing on the
    Leaderboard until an Admin lifts it. A Match you are in the middle of ends, and lifting the
    Suspension does not bring it back. Your Posts stay, and your XP and Streak are frozen. Only
    Admins can suspend.
  - **Removal** is final. See *Leaving 21 Beirut* in the Privacy Policy for what it keeps and what
    it deletes.
- **Admins can edit your profile fields** — display name, email address, avatar, Skills and Learning
  Goal — for example to remove an offensive name. They can never change your password, your XP or
  Badges, or your Posts, Reviews, Reports or Direct Messages.
- **There is no formal appeal.** An Admin can lift a Suspension; nobody can reverse a Removal.

To leave 21 Beirut yourself, ask an Admin to remove your account.

## 8. No guarantees

21 Beirut is provided as it is. A copy runs only while the computer running it does, and the team's
copy also depends on Supabase, which can pause it until the team resumes it. 21 Beirut keeps no
backups the team can restore, and data in a copy can be lost. It may contain mistakes. Neither its
authors nor whoever runs your copy promise that it will be available, accurate or suitable for any
purpose.
